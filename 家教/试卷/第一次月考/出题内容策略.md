## 一、先定边界：哪些分值得花题去补

> 依据 [[家教/试卷/第一次月考/试卷分析.md|试卷分析]] 的失分结构与 H1–H6 诊断。出题只投向"**可迁移、可验证、单位时间提分高**"的点。

| 失分项   |  可确认失分 | 是否出题  | 出题理由 / 替代做法                 |
| ----- | -----: | :---: | --------------------------- |
| 完形填空  | **12** | ✅ 重点  | 8 错里 6 错在"词和搭配"，换语境可复现      |
| 情景对话  |      4 |   ✅   | 方法性失分，5 题就能纠正               |
| 概要补全  |    4–6 |   ✅   | `by doing`、`ask for` 两个结构必考 |
| 任务型阅读 |    3–5 |   ✅   | 只练"完整句"，不练长篇                |
| 完成句子  |    2–4 |   ✅   | 句型转换是纯规则题，性价比高              |
| 语法选择  |      3 |   ✅   | 三个零散搭配点，10 题即可覆盖            |
| 听力    |      5 |   ❌   | 本轮不做听力；21、23 的拼写问题并入 M7 拼写专项 |
| 阅读理解  |    2–4 |   ❌   | 已是优势，**只保留 1 篇"拟标题"维持手感**   |

> [!tip] 结论
> 100 道新题里，**约 70 道直接对应本次错题考点**，20 道同考点换语境，10 道迁移。不做"大而全"的套卷，做"小而准"的题包。

## 二、三条命题原则

1. **一题一考点**：不出一道题同时考时态 + 搭配 + 拼写。错了才知道错在哪。
2. **干扰项 = 学生的真实错答**：`open up`、`how much`、`percent's`、`better`、`other` 必须原样出现在选项里，让"旧错误"再犯一次，才能被纠正。
3. **输出题按句给分，评分标准前置**：任务型阅读、概要补全、翻译，先告诉学生"主谓齐全才得分"，再动笔。

## 四、分模块细则与样题

### M1 · 词组块专项（对应 H1 ★★★）

**考点清单（20 个必背块）**

`find out` · `open up` · `look up` · `look after` · `look for` · `think about` · `ask sb for sth` · `ask sb to do sth` · `be afraid of` · `by doing` · `do well in/at` · `spend time doing` · `It takes sb time to do` · `help sb with sth` · `be interested in` · `one... the other...` · `keep doing` · `turn... into` · `join in` · `with`（带有）

**题型配比**：选择 10 + 介词/副词填空 5 + 改错 5 + 造句 5（造句放在课后口头/书面作业）。

**样题（选择）**

1. Please ___ the new word in the dictionary.
   A. look up　B. look after　C. look for　D. look at
2. The little girl is afraid ___ dogs.
   A. of　B. at　C. for　D. with
3. If you always ___ unhappy things, you can never relax yourself.
   A. think　B. think about　C. think over　D. think up
4. You can improve your English ___ reading every day.
   A. by　B. with　C. in　D. on
5. My teacher asks me ___ my homework on time.
   A. finish　B. finishing　C. to finish　D. finished
6. The police finally ___ the truth.
   A. found out　B. looked for　C. opened up　D. asked for
7. I usually get ___ seven hours of sleep every night.
   A. about　B. to　C. at　D. for
8. One of the twins is a doctor, and ___ is a teacher.
   A. other　B. the other　C. another　D. others
9. He often helps me ___ my English.
   A. with　B. for　C. at　D. on
10. I spend half an hour ___ English every morning.
    A. read　B. reading　C. to read　D. reads

**样题（介词/副词填空）**

1. I'm afraid ___ making mistakes.
2. She asked me ___ help.
3. We should think ___ others first.
4. He found ___ the answer at last.
5. You can keep healthy ___ doing exercise every day.

**样题（改错，每句一处）**

1. Thank you for help me.
2. He is afraid to dogs.
3. She asked his parents help.
4. Please look up it in the dictionary.
5. I go to school by walk.

> [!example]- M1 参考答案
> **选择**：1 A　2 A　3 B　4 A　5 C　6 A　7 A　8 B　9 A　10 B
> **填空**：1 of　2 for　3 about　4 out　5 by
> **改错**：1 help → helping　2 to → of　3 help → for help　4 look up it → look it up　5 by walk → on foot

### M2 · 完形填空专项（对应 H2 ★★★ + H3 部分）

**命题规格**

- 每篇 10 空，话题限定在 **睡眠 / 健康 / 学习习惯**（与本次完形同域）。
- 空位类型固定：疑问词 1、动词/形容词精确度 3、介词搭配 2、逻辑连词 1、限定词 1、短语动词 1、上下文反义 1。
- **每空必须有唯一依据句**，且依据句可以在上一句或下一句。讲评时要求学生指出来。
- 四步法印在卷首：通读 → 判词性 → 代入朗读 → 复读查逻辑。

**样篇（10 空，可整篇使用）**

> Many students have trouble sleeping before exams. Do you know __1__ you should sleep every night? Doctors say teenagers need eight to ten hours. But some students only get six.
>
> One reason is the phone. Many students play games in bed. The light from the phone makes their brains __2__. They think games can help them __3__, but in fact they become more excited. A study shows that students __4__ phones in their bedrooms sleep less.
>
> When we are asleep, our brains do not __5__ working. They are busy putting the day's information in order. So a good sleep helps us remember things better. Two groups of students took part in a test. One group listened to soft music before bed, and __6__ group didn't. The result showed that music helped students fall asleep faster. It also helped them do __7__ at school.
>
> If you want to __8__ why sleep is so important, read more about it. Go to bed early, __9__ you will feel full of energy tomorrow. And remember: don't use your phone for __10__ than 30 minutes before bed.

| 空 | 选项 | 考点 |
| :-: | --- | --- |
| 1 | A. how often　B. how long　C. how much　D. how soon | 疑问词 |
| 2 | A. quiet　B. active　C. relaxed　D. bored | 形容词精确度 |
| 3 | A. relax　B. interest　C. study　D. work | 动词精确度 |
| 4 | A. with　B. on　C. for　D. of | 介词搭配 |
| 5 | A. stop　B. keep　C. start　D. finish | 上下文反义 |
| 6 | A. other　B. the other　C. another　D. others | 限定词 |
| 7 | A. good　B. well　C. better　D. best | 副词/搭配 |
| 8 | A. find out　B. open up　C. look for　D. ask for | 短语动词 |
| 9 | A. or　B. and　C. but　D. so | 逻辑连词 |
| 10 | A. more　B. less　C. fewer　D. much | 比较结构 |

> [!example]- M2 参考答案与依据句
> 1 **B**　问"每晚睡多久"，答语是时间段 → `how long`。依据：后句 eight to ten hours。
> 2 **B**　手机光让大脑"活跃"→ `active`。依据：后句 but in fact they become more excited。
> 3 **A**　学生以为游戏能帮他们"放松"→ `relax`。依据：but in fact 转折。
> 4 **A**　"带有手机的孩子"→ `with`。依据：students with phones。
> 5 **B**　睡着时大脑"仍在"工作→ `do not keep working`。依据：后句 They are busy...
> 6 **B**　one... the other... 两组中的另一组。
> 7 **B**　`do well at school`，无比较对象，不用比较级。
> 8 **A**　"弄明白原因"→ `find out`。
> 9 **B**　祈使句 + and + 结果。
> 10 **A**　`more than 30 minutes`。

> [!tip] 第二篇完形的命题规格（迁移检测用）
> 换话题为"学习计划"，10 空按同样类型分布；把第 5 空改成 `stops/keeps` 的变式，第 9 空改成 `or/and` 的变式，确保是"新句子、旧考点"。

### M3 · 近义词精确度（对应 H3 ★★★）

**题型**：句中二选一，12 题，限时 6 分钟。要求学生在选完后写出"为什么另一个不行"（口头即可）。

**样题**

1. How ___ (long / much) do you sleep every night?
2. How ___ (much / many) water do you drink every day?
3. How ___ (often / long) do you exercise? — Twice a week.
4. Soft music helps me ___ (relax / interest).
5. The story ___ (interests / relaxes) me a lot.
6. About 40 ___ (percent / percent's) of students walk to school.
7. He does ___ (good / well) in maths.
8. He is ___ (good / well) at maths.
9. It ___ (takes / spends) me an hour to read every day.
10. The test shows that they were ___ (right / great).
11. The book ___ (costs / takes) me 20 yuan.
12. I ___ (spent / paid) 20 yuan for the book.

> [!example]- M3 参考答案
> 1 long　2 much　3 often　4 relax　5 interests　6 percent　7 well　8 good　9 takes　10 right　11 costs　12 paid
>
> **讲评抓手**：每组只问一句"另一个词的中文是什么？它跟谁搭配？"——把"中文对应"升级为"词性 + 搭配"。

### M4 · 句型转换（对应 H5 + 句法 ★★）

**命题规格**：12 题，覆盖 4 类必考动作。

| 类型 | 题量 | 规则一句话 |
| --- | :-: | --- |
| be 动词提前 | 3 | 句中有 be，直接提前，不加 do/does |
| 助动词后原形 | 3 | didn't / doesn't / won't 后一律用原形 |
| 特殊疑问句语序 | 3 | 疑问词 + 一般疑问句语序 |
| 否定句（含 some→any） | 3 | 找准第一个助动词/be 后加 not |

**样题**

1. Colour walk is really good for our health.（改为一般疑问句）
2. I went to the park for a colour walk before.（改为否定句）
3. They are going to see a film during the walk.（对 **a film** 提问）
4. She does her homework every day.（改为一般疑问句）
5. Tom plays football after school.（改为否定句）
6. He was reading at 8 o'clock last night.（改为一般疑问句）
7. They were playing basketball then.（改为否定句）
8. I will visit my grandma tomorrow.（改为一般疑问句）
9. My father goes to work by bus.（对 **by bus** 提问）
10. She is going to be a doctor.（对 **a doctor** 提问）
11. The boy found out the answer.（改为一般疑问句）
12. There are some apples in the box.（改为否定句）
13. 连词成句：be / Let's / happy / and / this / join in / easy / activity

> [!example]- M4 参考答案
> 1. Is colour walk really good for our health?
> 2. I didn't go to the park for a colour walk before.
> 3. What are they going to see during the walk?
> 4. Does she do her homework every day?
> 5. Tom doesn't play football after school.
> 6. Was he reading at 8 o'clock last night?
> 7. They weren't playing basketball then.
> 8. Will you visit your grandma tomorrow?
> 9. How does your father go to work?
> 10. What is she going to be?
> 11. Did the boy find out the answer?
> 12. There aren't any apples in the box.
> 13. Let's be happy and join in this easy activity.

### M5 · 完整句输出（对应 H5 ★★）

#### 5.1 任务型阅读：4 问，全部要求完整句

**样篇（约 110 词）**

> Li Ming is a middle school student. He used to stay up late and play games on his phone. At that time, he often felt tired in class and did badly in exams. Last month, his teacher talked with him. The teacher told him to make a plan and go to bed before 10:30. Li Ming also started to run for 20 minutes every morning. Now he sleeps well and does better at school. He says, "A good plan and enough sleep make me full of energy."

1. Does Li Ming sleep well now?
2. What did Li Ming use to do before bed?
3. What did the teacher tell him to do?
4. Why does Li Ming feel full of energy now?

> [!example]- M5.1 参考答案（评分看主谓）
> 1. Yes, he does.
> 2. He used to stay up late and play games on his phone.
> 3. The teacher told him to make a plan and go to bed before 10:30.
> 4. Because he has a good plan and enough sleep.
>
> **扣分点**：只写 `yes`、只写短语、缺主语、答句时态与问句不一致。

#### 5.2 七选五：5 空，7 选项，强制"选一个划一个"

> A: Hi, Lucy. You look tired. What's wrong?
> B: (1) ___ I'm worried about the exam.
> A: (2) ___
> B: About four hours every night, but I still feel I don't have enough time.
> A: (3) ___ A good plan can help you use time better.
> B: (4) ___ I'll try tonight.
> A: (5) ___
> B: Yes, I think I will. Thank you.

A. You can try to make a plan first.
B. And what about you?
C. How long do you study every day?
D. That's a good idea.
E. I'm sorry to hear that.
F. Why not ask your teacher for help?
G. I have trouble sleeping before exams.

> [!example]- M5.2 参考答案
> 1 G　2 C　3 A　4 D　5 F　（多余：B、E）
>
> **操作要求**：每选一个，立即在原选项上划线。62/63 的连锁错位就是这么来的。

### M6 · 主旨 / 最佳标题（对应 H6 ★★）

**命题规格**：只出 2 篇，每篇 4 选项。选项必须包含"局部细节型"干扰项（如只概括某一段），训练"帽子要罩住全文"。

**样篇 1**

> Wang Wei is 15. He loves climbing. He started climbing with his father when he was seven. At first, he was afraid of high places. His father told him to look up, not down. Now Wang Wei climbs every weekend. He says climbing is not always easy. Sometimes he falls, but he never gives up. Last year, he climbed Mount Tai with his friends. "Climbing teaches me to be brave," he says.

A. How to Climb Safely　B. A Young Climbing Lover　C. Mount Tai　D. Wang Wei's Father

**样篇 2**

> Many people think that breakfast is the most important meal of the day. A study of 2,000 students shows that those who eat breakfast do better in class. Breakfast gives us energy after a long night. It also helps us think clearly. But some students skip breakfast because they want to sleep longer. Doctors say this is a bad habit. A healthy breakfast can be simple: an egg, some milk and a piece of bread.

A. A Simple Breakfast　B. Why Breakfast Matters　C. How to Sleep Longer　D. Students' Bad Habits

> [!example]- M6 参考答案
> 1 **B**　覆盖"人物 + 热爱 + 成长"，C/D 都是局部。
> 2 **B**　全文讲早餐"为什么重要"，A 只是结尾细节，C/D 是局部/反面。
>
> **检验动作**：选完后用不超过 8 个词复述"谁、做了什么、为什么"。

### M8 · 概要补全（对应 74–77）

**命题规格**：120 词短文 + 4 空，空位只考"固定结构 + 信息补全"，不考推理。

**样篇**

> David often makes plans but never finishes them. His teacher gave him some advice. First, he should turn his plans into __1__. Second, he can start with small things and finish them one by one. Third, he can use a timer. By __2__ a timer, he can focus on one thing for 25 minutes. Fourth, when he has problems, he should ask his parents __3__ help. With these tips, David finished his plan and had a good summer __4__.

> [!example]- M8 参考答案
> 1 actions　2 using　3 for　4 holiday
>
> **对应原题**：74（turn plans into actions）、75（by doing）、76（ask sb for help）、77（summer holiday）。

## 五、干扰项设计清单

> 每个干扰项都必须"像学生的旧答案"，否则测不出真问题。

| 学生旧错答 | 为什么会被选 | 正确答案 | 放在哪种题里 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| open up | 中文都记成"打开" | find out | 四选一 |
| how much | 和 how long 都记成"多少" | how long | 疑问词四选一 |
| percent's | 按名词所有格类推 | percent | 四种形式并列 |
| better | 见"好"就选比较级 | well | 句中无 than |
| other | 忽略 one... the other | the other | 与 another/others 同组 |
| interest | 与 relax 都记成"感觉" | relax | 同句二选一 |
| think | 中文"想"不需要介词 | think about | 缺介词填空 |
| ask sb help | 中文"要帮助"直接对应 | ask sb for help | 改错 |
| use a timer | 不知道 by 后接 doing | by using | 介词后动词形式 |
| stops | 只看空格所在句 | keeps | 上下句逻辑 |
| or | 不熟"祈使句 + and" | and | 同句二选一 |
| did went | 只记得过去时 | did go | 否定句改写 |

| 词组块（选择+填空） | 15 | ≥13 | 回到 20 个词组卡，不刷新题 |
| 完形 | 10 | ≥8 | 同篇重做 + 标依据句 |
| 近义词 | 12 | ≥10 | 每组写 2 个对比句 |
| 句型转换 | 12 | ≥10 | 只讲错的那一类，再出 5 题同类 |
| 完整句 | 4 | 4 句均主谓齐全 | 逐句改写，标出主语谓语 |
| 七选五 | 5 | 5/5 | 重做并强制划选项 |
| 标题 | 2 | 2/2 | 口头复述"谁+做了什么" |
| 概要补全 | 4 | ≥3 | 只补 `by doing` / `ask for` 两个结构 |
| 拼写 | 15 | ≥13 | 错词隔天重测 |

> [!warning] 验收原则
> **不达标不升级**：同一考点连续两次达标，才进入下一模块；否则只换语境、不换考点。
> 所有错题必须回答一句"我错在哪一步"（词义 / 搭配 / 上下文 / 形式），说不出来就说明没真懂。

---

## 七、命题红线：这些题不要出

1. **不再加阅读理解**——已是 87%–93% 的优势项，边际收益最低，只保留 1 篇"拟标题"。
2. **不出超纲语法**——定语从句、被动语态、虚拟语气本阶段不考，出了只会打击信心。
3. **不出孤立单词拼写**——拼写必须放在句子或句中填词里，否则测的是默写不是运用。
4. **不出"一词多空"综合题**——一题一考点，否则错因无法归因。
5. **完形生词率不超过 5%**，且每空必须有唯一依据句；没有依据句的空一律删掉。
6. **不考偏怪近义词**——只考本次暴露的 6 组（how long/how much、relax/interest、percent、well/better、other/the other、takes/spends）。
7. **不把原题原样再印一遍**——至少换语境、换人名、换话题，避免"背答案"。

## 八、两周执行节奏

| 时间 | 内容 | 题包 | 时长 |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 第 1 次课前 | 诊断卷 A | A | 20′ |
