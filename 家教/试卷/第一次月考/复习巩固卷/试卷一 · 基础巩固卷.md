## 四、阅读理解（5 小题，每题 2 分，共 10 分）

Beijing Opera is China's national quintessence (国粹). It's one of the most important opera styles in China. It appeared in the late 18th century and became popular by the mid-19th century. Although it is called Beijing Opera, the origins (起源) of it are not in Beijing but in Anhui and Hubei. Sheng, Dan, Jing and Chou are four main roles in Beijing Opera.

**No.1 Sheng (生)**
Sheng is an important role in Beijing Opera and it is for men. This role mainly includes Laosheng, Xiaosheng, Hongsheng, Wawasheng and Wusheng. Laosheng is for the older role.

**No.2 Dan (旦)**
Dan is for the woman role in Beijing Opera. It includes Zhengdan, Huadan, Wudan, Daomadan and so on.

**No.3 Jing (净)**
Jing is a painted face for the man role. It can be the primary role or the secondary role and it usually has a long beard (胡须). It includes Zhengjing, Fujing, Wujing, Maojing and so on.

**No.4 Chou (丑)**
Chou is a man clown (小丑) role with a painted face. It is a funny role in Beijing Opera. It mainly includes Wenchou and Wuchou.

1. How long is the history of Beijing Opera by now?
   A. About 100 years.　B. More than 100 years.　C. More than 200 years.　D. Less than 200 years.
2. Beijing Opera may come from ___.
   A. Anhui and Hubei　B. Beijing　C. Shandong　D. Jiangsu
3. How many roles about Sheng does the writer mention (提到)?
   A. Three.　B. Four.　C. Five.　D. Six.
4. What do we call a woman role in Beijing Opera?
   A. Sheng.　B. Dan.　C. Jing.　D. Chou.
5. In which part of a magazine can we read the passage?
   A. Funny Stories.　B. Chinese Cities.　C. Chinese Stories.　D. Chinese Culture.

## 五、五选五（5 小题，每题 2 分，共 10 分）

A goat and a donkey lived together. As the donkey often took heavy things, his owner always gave him better food. (1) ___ The goat was unhappy because the owner didn't give him better food. (2) ___

One day, he told the donkey, "Oh, how poor you are! The owner either wants you to work hard or makes you carry heavy bags. You don't even have time to take a rest."

The donkey said, "(3) ___"

"Here is an idea. Why don't you pretend to be ill and fall over on purpose (故意地)? (4) ___"

Thus, the donkey pretended to be sick, fell into a hole and got hurt.

When his owner sent him for a vet (兽医), the vet told the master, "(5) ___" Hearing this, in order to cure the donkey, the owner killed the goat right away.

A. I know, but what can I do?
B. Then, you can take a good rest.
C. But the goat could only eat grass every day.
D. Then, he wanted to do some bad things to the donkey.
E. If you want him to get well soon, you have to feed him a goat's heart.

## 七、任务型阅读：完整句作答（4 小题，每题 2 分，共 8 分）

Marina and Mia are twins. Even their parents find it hard to tell the difference between them. Although they look the same, the girls are different in most other ways. They don't have many things in common. Mia likes hot food, while Marina prefers sweet food. Mia likes to study at night, while Marina prefers the morning.

They often fight with each other about these things. For example, when Mia says that waking up early in the morning is terrible, Marina argues, "Mornings are fresh. It is difficult to stay up late at night when I feel sleepy!"

Their parents decided to put an end to their arguments and teach them that they both could be correct.

Their father asked Mia to stand on one side of a board, while Marina stood on the other side. They couldn't see each other's side. Their mother asked, "What color is the board?" Mia said it was black, while Marina said it was white. They started arguing again. Their parents asked them to change sides.

They were both quite surprised. Mia's side was black and Marina's was white. Both of them were correct.

Many of us are like Mia and Marina. We may say that we are right most of the time, but never forget that others can be right as well.

**请用完整句回答，每题不少于 5 个词。**

1. What do Mia and Marina look like?
2. What food does Mia like?
3. Why did their parents ask them to stand on different sides of a board?
4. What can we learn from the story?
