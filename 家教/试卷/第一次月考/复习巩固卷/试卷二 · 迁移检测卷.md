## 三、完形填空（2 篇，每空 1 分，共 20 分）

### A

Hua Tuo was a famous doctor in ancient China. He cured (治愈) many __1__ people.

One day, Ni Xun and Li Yan went to see Hua Tuo. They __2__ had a headache and ran a fever. After a careful examination, Hua Tuo __3__ them two different kinds of medicines.

"Ni Xun, you should __4__ one kind of medicine to empty your bowels (肠)," Hua Tuo said.

"As for Li Yan, take __5__ kind of medicine to break into a sweat (开始出汗)."

The two men felt __6__. They asked, "We have the same illness. __7__ do you give us different medicines?"

"Because Ni Xun has too much heat inside his body, __8__ Li Yan catches a cold. The causes are different. Treatments (治疗) should be different, too," answered Hua Tuo.

The two men went back home and took __9__ medicines. They got well soon.

A __10__ gives medicine to a sick person according to his or her illness. In our life, we should also solve problems in different ways.

1. A. sick　B. old　C. young　D. kind
2. A. all　B. either　C. both　D. none
3. A. asked　B. bought　C. did　D. gave
4. A. sell　B. take　C. make　D. want
5. A. other　B. others　C. another　D. the other
6. A. surprised　B. happy　C. sad　D. excited
7. A. How　B. Where　C. Why　D. When
8. A. and　B. but　C. so　D. or
9. A. his　B. her　C. its　D. their
10. A. doctor　B. teacher　C. writer　D. worker

### B

During a flight for animals, a mouse was surprised to see a parrot sitting next to him. He didn't like parrots at all because he thought __1__ always talked too much and did too little.

After the plane __2__, the mouse asked the zebra stewardess (女乘务员) for a cup of coffee. At the same time, the parrot said __3__, "And get me a cola! Be quick!"

The zebra stewardess was a little __4__. She brought back a cola for the parrot but __5__ the coffee.

As the mouse told the zebra stewardess to bring the __6__ once more, the parrot finished the cola and shouted loudly again, "Get me __7__ cola!"

The zebra stewardess came back with another cola, __8__ still no coffee. The mouse was angry. He shouted __9__ the parrot, "I've asked you twice for coffee. Go and get it now!"

This made the zebra stewards (男乘务员) on the __10__ angry. The next moment, two zebra stewards threw both the mouse and the parrot out of the emergency exit (紧急出口).

1. A. they　B. he　C. it　D. she
2. A. put off　B. turned up　C. took off　D. broke down
3. A. quickly　B. loudly　C. slowly　D. quietly
4. A. happy　B. excited　C. relaxed　D. nervous
5. A. brought　B. made　C. forgot　D. took
6. A. coffee　B. tea　C. cola　D. milk
7. A. other　B. another　C. the other　D. others
8. A. so　B. or　C. and　D. but
9. A. about　B. with　C. over　D. like
10. A. plane　B. bus　C. car　D. taxi

## 四、阅读理解（5 小题，每题 2 分，共 10 分）

Paper is one of the most important products ever invented by man. The invention of paper meant that more people could get a good education because people could print more books. Paper provided an important way to spread (传播) information.

According to Chinese historical records, Cai Lun, an official during the Han Dynasty, made paper successfully in about 105 A.D. He took the inner bark (树皮) of a tree and bamboo, mixed them with water, and pounded them with a wooden tool. He then put this mixture onto a piece of cloth and let the water through, leaving only the fibers (纤维) on the cloth. After it dried, Cai Lun discovered that he had created a good writing thing. It was easy to make and carry.

People introduced Chinese papermaking to Vietnam and later to Japan in the beginning, but the papermaking process was kept secret in Silk Road trading.

During the Tang Dynasty and the Song Dynasty, many kinds of paper appeared, including bamboo paper and Xuan paper.

By the end of the 7th century, the papermaking process reached India, Nepal, Pakistan, and Bangladesh. About in the 11th century, the invention got to Europe.

Today, both paper and paper money are used in all corners.

1. Because of the invention of paper, more people ___ according to the passage.
   A. could get a good education　B. became richer and richer　C. needed to plant more trees　D. developed many kinds of paper
2. The underlined word "pounded" in Paragraph 2 means ___ in Chinese.
   A. 挥动　B. 捣碎　C. 碰撞　D. 推动
3. Which is the right order to make paper according to historical records?
   ① Leave the fibers on the cloth and dry it.
   ② Mix the inner bark and bamboo with water and pound them.
   ③ Take some inner bark of a tree and bamboo.
   ④ Put the mixture onto a piece of cloth.
   A. ②①③④　B. ②①④③　C. ③②①④　D. ③②④①
4. Which of the following is TRUE according to the passage?
   A. Bamboo paper and Xuan paper appeared during the Han Dynasty.
   B. In Silk Road trading, people kept the papermaking process secret.
   C. At the end of the 7th century, the paper invention appeared in Europe.
   D. At the beginning of the 7th century, papermaking reached India, Nepal and other countries.
5. What's the best title for the passage?
   A. How to Make Paper　B. How to Spread Papermaking　C. Cai Lun's Making Paper　D. The Ways of Spreading Paper

## 七、任务型阅读：完整句作答（4 小题，每题 2 分，共 8 分）

Long ago, there was a man named Huang Fu. He was very rich and had a lot of money. But he wasn't friendly at all. One day, he gave a bottle to his farmhand (农场工人) Hop and said, "Buy me a bottle of alcohol."

"But you didn't give me any money," Hop said in surprise. "How can I buy you alcohol with no money?"

Huang Fu shouted angrily, "Anyone can buy alcohol with money. But it takes real skills to buy alcohol with no money." Hop said nothing and went out. He thought for a moment and got a good idea.

An hour later, Hop got back with an empty bottle. He gave the bottle to Huang Fu and said, "Enjoy the alcohol, sir."

Looking at the bottle, Huang Fu was very angry and shouted, "There is no alcohol. How can I enjoy it?"

"Anyone can enjoy alcohol if there is some," Hop said. "It takes real skills to enjoy alcohol when there is nothing."

Huang Fu's face turned red and said nothing.

**请用完整句回答，每题不少于 5 个词。**

1. What did Huang Fu ask Hop to do?
2. Why did Hop bring back an empty bottle?
3. What kind of person is Hop?
4. What can we learn from the story?

## 八、概要补全（4 空，每空 2 分，共 8 分）

First, she should make a __1__ (计划) and finish her homework earlier. Second, she can relax by __2__ (听) to soft music before bed. Third, if she has problems, she can ask her parents __3__ (寻求) help. With enough sleep, Lucy does __4__ (好) at school than before.
