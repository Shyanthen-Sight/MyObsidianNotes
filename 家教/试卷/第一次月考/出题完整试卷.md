## 四、阅读理解（5 小题，每题 2 分，共 10 分）

Beijing Opera is China's national quintessence (国粹). It's one of the most important opera styles in China. It appeared in the late 18th century and became popular by the mid-19th century. Although it is called Beijing Opera, the origins (起源) of it are not in Beijing but in Anhui and Hubei. Sheng, Dan, Jing and Chou are four main roles in Beijing Opera.

**No.1 Sheng (生)**
Sheng is an important role in Beijing Opera and it is for men. This role mainly includes Laosheng, Xiaosheng, Hongsheng, Wawasheng and Wusheng. Laosheng is for the older role.

**No.2 Dan (旦)**
Dan is for the woman role in Beijing Opera. It includes Zhengdan, Huadan, Wudan, Daomadan and so on.

**No.3 Jing (净)**
Jing is a painted face for the man role. It can be the primary role or the secondary role and it usually has a long beard (胡须). It includes Zhengjing, Fujing, Wujing, Maojing and so on.

**No.4 Chou (丑)**
Chou is a man clown (小丑) role with a painted face. It is a funny role in Beijing Opera. It mainly includes Wenchou and Wuchou.

   A. Three.　B. Four.　C. Five.　D. Six.
