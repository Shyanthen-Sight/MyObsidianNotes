## A　Why Do We Sweat?

科普说明文　约 210 词　难度 ★★★★☆

In summer days, you might often hear people say that they're "sweating buckets". What are they talking about? Of course, they don't have buckets coming out of their skin, but they are probably sweating a lot.

Sweating is your body's way of cooling itself. When your body does, it produces (产生) heat. Whether you are exercising, eating or even sleeping, your body produces heat all the time.

Your body needs to keep the temperature of about 37℃. And the temperature of your body should always be the same if you are fine, no matter whether the weather is hot or cold. That is why the doctor takes your temperature when you are sick.

During hot summer months, your body temperature can rise quickly. To keep yourself from overheating, your body uses your blood and skin to get rid of excess (过多的) heat. As your blood heats up, it carries the heat to the skin. This causes your sweat glands (汗腺) to produce sweat.

As sweat comes out of the tiny holes on your skin, it evaporates (蒸发) and cools the skin. One drop of sweat can cool one liter of blood by about 0.5℃.

Your body is losing water as you sweat. Therefore, it's important to drink plenty of water and stay well hydrated (保持体内水分充足).

**1.** Which of the following is TRUE according to the passage?

A. Your body stops producing heat when you are asleep.

B. Sweat cools the skin as it evaporates from the tiny holes on it.

C. Blood carries sweat from the skin to the rest of the body.

D. One drop of sweat can lower the temperature of the blood by about 5℃.

## B　The Ice Block and the Treasure

寓言故事　约 225 词　难度 ★★★☆☆

There was an ice block in the middle of the forest one morning. It was a big block of ice. But what interested the animals most was the treasure they could see inside it.

As soon as they saw it, all the animals ran towards the block, trying to get the treasure out. But there was a weasel (鼬鼠). He didn't move.

The elephant was using his body to hit the block. Several monkeys, thinking the block was made of cold water, tried to lick the block away with their tongues (舌头). All the animals were trying to break the block, but ended up failing.

However, just then someone said, "Look! Something is moving inside the block!"

In fact, it was the weasel. He soon came out with the treasure. They were all surprised to see how the weasel had gotten the treasure without breaking the block.

The weasel told them that before doing that he had taken some time to think and watch. He thought he could get in under the block by digging a tunnel (隧道). At the end of the tunnel he built a small fire. It quickly melted a hole in the ice.

The weasel did it by showing everyone that one can achieve more by thinking about problems rather than by jumping into action at once.

**2.** Which of the following best matches the message of the story?

A. Hard work always pays off in the end.

B. Teamwork makes the impossible possible.

C. The largest animal is the most powerful one.

D. A calm mind works better than a strong body.

## C　Lisa and the Escaped Prisoner

记叙文（悬疑）　约 230 词　难度 ★★★★☆

Lisa was born and grew up in a small town. She was a great make-up artist (化妆师) and owned a shop on a busy street.

One morning, she arrived at her shop early, but no customers (顾客) came. She turned on the TV and began to watch the morning news.

To her surprise, there was a piece of big news that day. A bad person had escaped from prison (监狱) the night before and hidden in the town. The police were doing their best to catch the person.

Lisa turned off the TV and wanted to do some cleaning. But suddenly, a man ran into her shop. Looking at him carefully, Lisa realized he was just that bad person. She was so afraid.

"What...what do...you...want, sir?" she asked.

"I want you to make me up! I don't want the police to catch me. Do it quickly, or I'll kill you!" the man said.

Lisa had to follow his orders. Half an hour later, she finished. The person looked at himself in the mirror and said, "You did a really good job. I don't even recognize myself!"

The bad person then walked out of the shop. Out on the street, he thought that no one would recognize him. However, two policemen soon came near and caught him.

How did the police find the bad person?

**3.** The story ends with the question "How did the police find the bad person?" The most possible answer is that ______.

A. the make-up didn't change his looks at all

B. Lisa had secretly reported him to the police while making him up

C. Lisa told everyone in town about the man

D. he turned himself in because he felt sorry

## D　My Tea Adventure

文化散文　约 230 词　难度 ★★★★☆

When I was a child, my aunt liked making southern-style sweet tea, but I never really drank much tea as an adult until recently.

After reading about its health benefits (好处), I thought I'd start drinking tea more often. Over the last several months, I have found the joy of drinking tea in all its different forms. I've mostly been drinking Chinese tea, but I've tried some others as well.

As far as Chinese tea goes, my favorites are black tea and chrysanthemum (菊花) tea. Chrysanthemum tea has a light and sweet taste, while black tea gives me the energy I need to get through the day.

Speaking of black tea, you'd be surprised at how common it is in other parts of the world. Remember that southern-style sweet tea I mentioned? Although it's an American drink, it uses black tea as its base (基础成分). It is sweetened with sugar and fruit. And unlike black tea in China, people usually drink sweet tea with ice.

I tried British tea as well. Most Chinese people make tea with loose (零散的) leaves, but in Britain, people use tea bags. They like to add milk to it, but I like the pure (纯粹的) taste of tea more.

This is just the start of my tea adventure (冒险). I'm sure there are even more flavors and types that are just waiting for me to try.

**4.** Why does the writer mention southern-style sweet tea and British tea in Paragraphs 4 and 5?

A. To show how black tea is enjoyed in different parts of the world.

B. To prove that Chinese tea is better than foreign tea.

C. To explain why sweet tea should be drunk with ice.

D. To teach readers how to make tea with tea bags.

## E　How to Write a Movie Review

说明文（写作指导）　约 230 词　难度 ★★★★☆

As a student, your teacher may ask you to write a movie review (评论). At first it may seem like a piece of cake, but when you actually sit down to write it, you may find it hard. Here are the steps for you.

**Get Basic Information of the Movie**　Get basic information like the movie plot (情节), director, subject and places. Find more information about the story and background (背景) on the subject.

**Watch the Movie Twice**　Before you start to write the review, you need to watch the movie twice. While doing so, you need to make the best use of your sense of observation (观察力). Write down all the important information so that you can always refer to it.

**Make an Outline (大纲) for Your Review**　Now that the pre-writing part is realized, the real job begins—writing the movie review. Start by introducing the movie simply. Use the basic writing rule here: Begin your writing with a phrase or short sentence. I personally advise you to use a conversation or sentence from the movie which shows its subject.

**Finish Your Review**　Following the outline, you can write your review more easily. What you need to do is adding more information to every part. During writing, you can get help from previous (先前的) reviews. You can make your review interesting by comparing the plot of the movie with that of one similar movie, giving special explanations of good parts or adding different thoughts to the movie.

**5.** How is the passage mainly organized?

A. By comparing good reviews with bad ones.

B. By telling a story that happens in a school.

C. By listing the steps of writing a review in order.

D. By asking and answering questions about movies.

## 参考答案与解析

**答案速查**

| 题号 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **答案** | **B** | **D** | **B** | **A** | **C** |

### 1. B　细节辨析题（A 篇）

**定位**：第 5 段 "As sweat comes out of the tiny holes on your skin, it evaporates (蒸发) and cools the skin."

**思路**：四项都要回原文逐条核对，三个干扰项分别埋在第 2、4、5 段：

- A 错在时间——第 2 段说 "Whether you are exercising, eating or even sleeping, your body produces heat all the time"，睡着时也在产热。
- C 错在方向——第 4 段是血液把**热量**带到皮肤，汗是**汗腺**分泌的，不是血液"运汗"。
- D 错在数字——原文是 "by about 0.5℃"，选项换成 5℃。

**干扰项**：A、C 都是"半对半错"的改写，只有把范围副词（all the time）和动作方向（heat → skin）看清楚才能排除。

### 2. D　主旨寓意题（B 篇）

**定位**：末段 "one can achieve more by thinking about problems rather than by jumping into action at once."

**思路**：大象以身撞冰、猴子以舌舔冰都失败了，安静的鼬鼠靠"观察—挖隧道—生火融冰"拿到宝物。D 项"冷静的头脑胜过强壮的身体"是原文寓意的高级同义改写。

**干扰项**：A"努力总有回报"——动物们足够努力却全部失败，与文意相反；B"团队合作"——文中动物各自为战，鼬鼠也是独自完成；C"体格最强者最有力量"与主旨正好相反。

### 3. B　开放式结尾推断题（C 篇）

**定位**：需整合三条线索：① Lisa 在电视新闻里认出他就是越狱逃犯；② Lisa 被迫为他化妆，独处了整整半小时；③ 他一走出店门 "two policemen soon came near and caught him"——警察来得又快又准。

**思路**：妆容效果极好（他自己都"认不出自己"），警方却能在第一时间定位，最合理的解释是 Lisa 在化妆期间悄悄报了警，故选 B。

**干扰项**：A 与原文 "I don't even recognize myself!" 直接矛盾；C 无任何依据；D"自首"与 "bad person" 的人物设定不符。

### 4. A　写作目的题（D 篇）

**定位**：第 4 段主题句 "you'd be surprised at how common it is in other parts of the world"，随后以美式甜茶为例；第 5 段又补英国茶（茶包＋加奶）一例。

**思路**：两个例子共同服务于同一论点——同一款红茶在不同国家有完全不同的喝法，故 A 正确。

**干扰项**：B"证明中国茶更好"——作者通篇都在欣赏各地茶，并无比较优劣；C、D 都只是段落里的细节，不是举例目的。

### 5. C　篇章结构题（E 篇）

**定位**：四个加粗小标题：Get Basic Information → Watch the Movie Twice → Make an Outline → Finish Your Review。

**思路**：首段总起 "Here are the steps for you"，之后按写影评的操作顺序逐段展开，是典型的"步骤顺序"组织方式，故选 C。

**干扰项**：A"对比好坏影评"、B"讲故事"、D"问答式"均与文本结构不符；D 有一定迷惑性——首段虽有对话口吻，但全文主体是步骤说明。

### 讲评建议

1. **先问依据句**：五道题都能在原文找到唯一依据，讲评时让学生先指句子，再讲选项，避免"凭感觉选对"。
2. **A、D 两篇最容易掉分**：A 篇三个干扰项分别改"时间、方向、数字"，D 篇考"举例为谁服务"，这两类正是中考细节题与写作目的题的高频坑。
3. **B、C 两篇练推断**：B 篇要求把寓言抽象成道理，C 篇要求补出文本没写的行为，学生若只答原文出现过的信息，说明还停在"找词"层面。
4. **错题归档**：错哪题就归到"细节辨析 / 主旨寓意 / 推断 / 写作目的 / 结构"五类中的一类，累计两次同类型错误，再补 2 篇同类语篇。
